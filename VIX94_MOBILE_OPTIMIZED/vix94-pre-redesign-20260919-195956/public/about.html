ABOUT VIX 94'

Independent music.
Curated frequency.

VIX 94' is an independent online radio platform built around music discovery, handpicked listening and emerging artists. The idea is simple: create a distinctive frequency for people who want to hear something beyond the obvious.

The Radio

VIX 94' is the home of the live listening experience: a continuously evolving selection of music presented through a minimal, visual radio interface.

Music Discovery

The platform is designed to surface tracks, artists and sounds that deserve another listen — from emerging artists to established records that still belong on the frequency.

For Artists

VIX 94' is being built as a discovery point for independent artists who want their music heard in a curated radio environment.

What Comes Next

The platform will expand into artist features, music discovery, original releases, submissions and curated VIX 94' content.
