MUSIC SUBMISSION

Send your frequency.

VIX 94' is built around music discovery. Artists and independent projects can submit tracks for consideration in the station's curated rotation.

What to prepare

Please have the following information ready before submitting. A direct streaming link is preferred so the track can be reviewed easily.

  • Artist or project name
  • Track title
  • Genre or style
  • Country or city
  • Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube link
  • Instagram or social profile
  • Short artist or project bio
  • Direct track or private listening link
Complete the form and press Submit Music to prepare your submission email. This page does not upload files or store personal information on VIX 94'.
